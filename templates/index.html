Describe what happened and I'll classify the severity and give you immediate next steps. For example: "I got an email asking for my bank OTP" or "my files are locked and it's asking for payment."

How it works

  1. Describe the incident in plain language
  2. The rule engine matches keywords against 6 known categories
  3. You get a severity level and immediate response actions

Severity levels

  • Critical — escalate immediately
  • High — act within the hour
  • Medium — respond same day
  • Low — monitor / no action

Incident categories

Phishing Ransomware Unauthorized Access Malware Data Leak Lost / Stolen Device

Reference framework

Classification logic modeled on the Detection & Analysis phase of NIST SP 800-61, the Computer Security Incident Handling Guide used by real SOC teams.
